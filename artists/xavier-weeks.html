Biography

Xavier Weeks is a genre-defying artist whose sound blends the warmth of analog production with contemporary electronic textures. Raised between coasts, his music carries the weight of displacement and the freedom of reinvention. Since signing with Akasha Music, Xavier has built a devoted following through intimate live performances and a string of critically acclaimed releases.

His work draws from a deep well of influence — ambient music, folk storytelling, and the textural richness of film scores — resulting in songs that feel both cinematic and deeply personal. Whether performing in a packed venue or an empty church, Xavier brings the same unguarded intensity to every note.

"Music isn't just sound. It's a world you build around yourself."

Following the success of his 2024 EP Night Drive, Xavier spent six months between studios in Reykjavik and Los Angeles, developing material that pushes further into electronic territory while retaining the raw vocal intimacy that first drew listeners in. The result is a body of work that refuses easy categorization — and an artist who continues to evolve with every release.

Xavier's live show has become a destination in its own right, blending improvisation with carefully constructed visual environments. Collaborations with lighting designers and visual artists have transformed his concerts into fully immersive experiences, earning critical praise and a growing international touring schedule.
